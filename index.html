LIFE HERE

Lakeside Living in Montgomery County

The French Quarter on Lake Conroe is in Willis, a small town set between Lake Conroe and the pines of the Sam Houston National Forest. It’s a quieter, lake-centered pace of life, with Conroe, The Woodlands, and Houston reachable by way of Interstate 45. [4]

01

Life on Lake Conroe

Lake Conroe covers more than 20,000 acres, and people come here to swim, kayak, boat, and fish. Anglers go after largemouth bass, channel catfish, crappie, and hybrid striped bass, and several public golf courses nearby, including one on the lake’s shore, keep things interesting on land.

SOURCES[1][2][3]
02

Connected by I-45

Willis sits just off Interstate 45, about 40 miles from Houston. The Woodlands, roughly 27 miles north of downtown Houston, lies along the same corridor to the south.

SOURCES[4][5]
03

Willis ISD Schools

Homes on this stretch of French Quarter Drive are within Willis Independent School District. Families can confirm the elementary and middle schools for a specific address on the district’s attendance-zone maps.

SOURCES[6][7]
04

Dining and Shopping

In historic downtown Conroe you’ll find antiques, galleries, live theatre at the Crighton and Owen theatres, and local restaurants. Farther south, The Woodlands offers open-air Market Street, The Woodlands Mall, and more than 150 restaurants.

SOURCES[8][9]
05

Forest and Trails

The Sam Houston National Forest covers about 163,000 acres and borders the northern part of the lake. It is home to the 129-mile Lone Star Hiking Trail, and Forest Service recreation areas such as Cagle and Scott’s Ridge have boat ramps on Lake Conroe.

SOURCES[10][11][3][2]
06

Texas Taxes, Plainly

The Texas Constitution prohibits a state tax on individuals’ net income, and Texas has no state property tax. Property taxes are set locally by the county, school district, and special districts, and they are the largest single source of funding for local services. Before you buy, review the current rates for a specific address with the Montgomery Central Appraisal District.

SOURCES[12][13][14]
