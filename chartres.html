Plan highlights

Built around one big vaulted room

01

Vaulted great room

The great room has a vaulted ceiling and a 72″ linear fireplace, and the vault carries out through the rear covered patio for a seamless indoor-outdoor feel.

02

Kitchen and dining

An open kitchen and dining room are designed around a large island with seating, with a walk-in pantry and a mud room on the way in from the garage.

03

Private primary suite

A spacious vaulted primary bedroom sits on its own side of the house, with a generous bath and a walk-in closet that connects to the utility room.

04

Flex / retreat

A 13′-wide flex room with a 10′ ceiling works as a home office, sitting room, fitness room or private getaway, reached from the great room or the primary bedroom.

05

Two more bedrooms

Bedrooms 2 and 3 share a hall bath with two sinks and a tub, at the front of the home.

06

2 or 3 cars

Choose a 2-car garage or add an optional third bay. The covered front porch has a 14′ ceiling, and the gallery hall inside has a 12′ ceiling.
